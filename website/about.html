About NivoStack

Building tools that mobile teams actually need.

Our Mission

NivoStack was founded with a simple belief: mobile developers shouldn't have to ship new builds just to debug issues or change configurations. We're building the infrastructure that lets you observe, debug, and remotely configure your mobile apps in real-time.

The Problem We Solve

Traditional mobile development cycles are slow. A bug in production means investigating logs, pushing a fix, waiting for app store review, and hoping users update. Feature flags require code changes. API debugging happens in the dark.

NivoStack changes this. With our SDK, you get instant visibility into what's happening in your app, the ability to change configurations without new builds, and tools that actually make debugging possible.
