Data Processing Agreement

Last updated: January 1, 2026

1. Introduction

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service between NivoStack ("Processor") and the customer ("Controller") for the processing of personal data.

2. Definitions

  • "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person
  • "Processing" means any operation performed on Personal Data
  • "Sub-processor" means any third party engaged by Processor to process Personal Data
  • "Data Protection Laws" means GDPR, CCPA, and other applicable privacy regulations

3. Scope of Processing

Processor will process Personal Data only:

  • To provide the Service as described in the Terms
  • In accordance with Controller's documented instructions
  • As required by applicable law

4. Data Categories

Categories of data that may be processed:

  • Device identifiers and technical metadata
  • API request/response data
  • Application logs and events
  • Any data Controller chooses to send via SDK
Controller's Responsibility: Controller is responsible for ensuring they have the legal basis to collect and process any Personal Data sent to the Service.

5. Security Measures

Processor implements appropriate technical and organizational measures including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Access controls and authentication requirements
  • Regular security assessments and audits
  • Incident response procedures
  • Employee training on data protection

6. Sub-processors

Current sub-processors:

Provider Purpose Location
Supabase Database, Authentication US/EU
Vercel Hosting US/EU
Stripe Payment Processing US

Controller will be notified of changes to sub-processors with 30 days advance notice.

7. Data Subject Rights

Processor will assist Controller in responding to data subject requests for:

  • Access to Personal Data
  • Rectification of inaccurate data
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Data portability
  • Restriction of processing

8. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a Personal Data breach, Processor will:

  • Notify Controller within 72 hours of becoming aware
  • Provide details of the nature and scope of the breach
  • Describe measures taken to address the breach
  • Cooperate with Controller's breach response

9. International Transfers

For transfers outside the EEA, Processor ensures appropriate safeguards through:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
  • Data Processing Addendums with sub-processors
  • EU-US Data Privacy Framework certification (where applicable)

10. Audit Rights

Controller may request:

  • Security certifications and audit reports
  • Responses to security questionnaires
  • On-site audits with reasonable notice (Enterprise plans)

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Upon termination of the Service:

  • Controller may export their data within 30 days
  • Processor will delete Personal Data within 90 days
  • Backups are purged according to retention schedule

12. Contact

Data Protection Officer: dpo@nivostack.com

For DPA inquiries: legal@nivostack.com
