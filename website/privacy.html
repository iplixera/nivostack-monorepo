Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Overview

NivoStack ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our services.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Name (optional)
  • Company name (optional)
  • Payment information (processed by Stripe)

Technical Data from Your Apps

When you integrate our SDK, we collect data you choose to send:

  • API request/response metadata (URLs, status codes, timing)
  • Device information (model, OS version, app version)
  • Custom events and logs you instrument
  • Configuration and feature flag states
Important: NivoStack does not collect personal information about your app's end users. You control what data is sent through our SDK.

How We Use Information

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Send service-related communications
  • Respond to support requests
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

Data Retention

We retain your data as follows:

  • Account data: Until you delete your account
  • API traces: 7 days (Free) or 30 days (Pro)
  • Logs and events: Based on your plan settings
  • Configurations: Until deleted by you

Data Security

We protect your data using:

  • TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • Regular security audits
  • Access controls and logging

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Export your data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt out of marketing communications

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Stripe: Payment processing
  • Supabase: Database and authentication
  • Vercel: Hosting

Contact Us

For privacy-related questions: privacy@nivostack.com

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via email or dashboard notification.
